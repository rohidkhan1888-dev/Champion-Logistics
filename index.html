Legal Framework

Terms of Service

1. Service Agreement: Champion Logistics Worldwide acts as an independent dispatch agent on behalf of licensed motor carriers. By signing our dispatch agreement, carriers authorize us to negotiate rates and handle logistics documentation.

2. No Forced Dispatch: The carrier retains ultimate decision-making power over every load offered. We will never obligate a driver to take a load they reject.

3. Payments & Brokerage: Brokers pay carriers directly. Champion Logistics Worldwide invoices carrier service fees according to agreed terms following successful rate confirmation issuance.

Data Protection

Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection: We collect business contact details, MC/USDOT numbers, insurance certificates, and equipment info exclusively for carrier onboarding and dispatch fulfillment.

2. Data Security: Champion Logistics Worldwide never sells or trades carrier data to third-party marketers. Information is strictly shared with verified brokers for rate setups.
